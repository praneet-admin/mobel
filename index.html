Questions

Before you send the first enquiry

How fast can you quote a lane?

Inside 24 hours on our core routes, and usually the same day if you send origin, destination, commodity and approximate volume together. Unusual cargo — hazardous, oversized, temperature-controlled — can take an extra day while we confirm handling.

What does “landed cost” actually include?

Freight, origin and destination handling, customs entry, duty and taxes, and final delivery. Anything genuinely unknowable at quote time — demurrage from a delay at your end, for example — is listed as an assumption rather than buried.

Do you handle customs yourselves?

Yes. Classification, valuation and entry are filed in-house by our own licensed brokers. That's the whole point of the company — nothing is handed to a third party you then have to chase separately.

Is there a minimum shipment size?

No minimum for freight — LCL consolidation makes part-pallet volumes workable. Wholesale supply starts at pallet quantities, since below that the import pricing stops being worth the paperwork for either of us.

Can you work with our existing suppliers?

Yes, and most of our clients start that way. We collect from your factory, prepare the documents with them directly, and take it from there. We can also vet new suppliers on lanes we already run.
