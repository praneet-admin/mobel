Questions

Before you place the first order

How fast can you quote an electronics order?

Inside 24 hours for categories we already source, and usually the same day if you send the model or spec, quantity, target price and destination together. New or custom products can take an extra day while we confirm suppliers and samples.

Which certifications can you handle?

BIS / CRS registration for India, CE and UKCA for Europe and the UK, FCC for the US, RoHS, and WPC approval for wireless devices. We file them in the importer's name and start while the goods are in production so nothing waits at the port.

Can you ship lithium batteries and battery-powered devices?

Yes. Power banks, phones, laptops and loose cells are packed, labelled and declared to IATA dangerous-goods rules (UN3480, UN3481, UN3091). Some loose-cell volumes have to move by sea — we tell you which before you buy.

What happens if units arrive dead or damaged?

Every order is sample-tested at the factory before final payment, so most problems are caught there. Anything dead on arrival is replaced under the supplier warranty we negotiate into the order, and cargo is insured door to door.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

It depends on the factory: usually one carton for accessories and around 100 units for phones or laptops. From our own stock in Dubai and Mumbai there is no minimum beyond a full carton.

Can you do private label or OEM branding?

Yes — logo, packaging, manuals and firmware splash screens, on most accessory and audio categories and on selected phones and tablets. Expect a higher MOQ and a few extra weeks of lead time.
